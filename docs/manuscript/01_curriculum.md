# Curriculum and streams

Spin follows the chapters of Parr, Pezzulo, and Friston's introduction to
active inference [@parr2022]. Each available module offers Pulse, Vision,
and Core. Pulse uses short conversational activities. Vision presents
spatial or animated interactions. Core develops formal ideas with rendered
equations and retrieval questions.

The module registry names each module and supplies its quiz entries and
glossary terms. The module route selects the stream from its lazy
imports. A learner can switch streams within a module. The route can
restore the previously selected stream, while the stream components
restore positions recorded for their individual paths [@spin-source].
